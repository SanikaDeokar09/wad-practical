Famous Tourist Places of India

Taj Mahal

Located in Agra, the Taj Mahal is one of the Seven Wonders of the World and symbolizes love.

Goa

Goa is famous for beaches, nightlife, seafood, and Portuguese culture.

Kerala

Kerala is known for backwaters, greenery, houseboats, and natural beauty.

Jaipur

Jaipur is known as the Pink City and is famous for forts and palaces.
