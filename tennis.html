Basic Rules of Tennis

  • A tennis match can be played in singles or doubles format.
  • Players score points as 15, 30, 40 and Game.
  • A player must win at least 6 games to win a set.
  • A match is usually best of 3 or best of 5 sets.
  • The ball must stay within court boundaries during play.
